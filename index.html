Care within reach

Quality care. More ways to afford it.

HealthLinc accepts Medicare, Medicaid, Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) and most commercial insurance plans. Patients without insurance are welcome, too.

Eligible patients may receive services through a sliding fee scale based on household income and size, regardless of insurance status.
